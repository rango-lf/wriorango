about me

Adult male-impersonating lesbian tomboy (he/him). Ill in the mind region. Often described by others as off-putting, odd, and dyke-ish to varying degrees. Undergrad industrial/systems engineer who really shouldn't become a professor, but is still in pursuit of becoming one regardless. I can often be found with a Casio Fx-991, a baseball hat, and a copy of the Iliad.


I spend most of my free time staring at screens, daydreaming, and not really liking myself. When I am not doing that, I enjoy reading fanfiction, creating new stories and characters, and learning about applied mathematics and artificial intelligence. I'm particularly interested in exploring ethics, policy, and public opinion in relation to AI and, more broadly, what it means for 21st-century society. I dream of turning this interest into an academic career.

about the site

Made with HTML, CSS, and a little bit of JS. Coded on a Thinkpad and a Yoga. Edited with VSCode and Codepen.io. Hosted and launched by Neocities.

The site name "Wriorango" originated from a ship name for myself and Wriothesley, a character from the RPG Genshin Impact--who was my initial inspiration for creating this site. Though that phase has passed, I continue to maintain this site to digitally immortalise who I was during this period of my life for whoever is unfortunate enough to find this. Kind of like a time capsule. Well, basically a time capsule.

My "site manifesto", if you will, includes prioritising ease of navigation, use, and maintanence. This is so that my site can focus on the important stuff: C-PTSD and autistic self-indulgence, off-putting writing, and propaganda for my interests. It aims to be a place where I can make my problems everyone else's problems, if you'll allow me.
